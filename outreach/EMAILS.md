# Email templates

Drafts only. Personalize every one, and send them from your own account. Keep them short.
One specific, honest claim beats a list of buzzwords.

---

## 1. Cold intro (maintenance / reliability manager)

**Subject:** Unplanned downtime on your [pumps / conveyors / rooftop units]?

Hi [First name],

I build predictive-maintenance software that reads the sensor data your equipment already
logs and warns before a failure. On 20 real bearings it had never seen before, it flagged
84% of faults while they were still developing.

I'm looking for one or two plants to run a free, no-commitment pilot: you share a sensor
export and your breakdown log, and within two weeks I'll show you how many of your *past*
failures it would have caught, and how many false alarms it would have raised. It can run
entirely on your own site, so your data never leaves.

Would a 15-minute call next week make sense?

[Your name]
[Phone] · [one-line link to the one-pager or live demo]

---

## 2. Follow-up (5–7 days later, no reply)

**Subject:** Re: Unplanned downtime on your [equipment]

Hi [First name],

Quick follow-up. The pilot is just a backtest on your own history: no install, no cost,
and you see the results before deciding anything. If [equipment] downtime isn't a pain
point right now, no problem. Is there someone else at [Company] I should ask?

[Your name]

---

## 3. Pilot proposal (after a good first call)

**Subject:** Pilot proposal: [Company] [equipment]

Hi [First name],

Thanks for the time today. Here's what we discussed:

- **Scope:** [N] [machine type] units at [site].
- **Data:** [sensor list] from [system], [period] of history, plus the breakdown log
  (template attached).
- **Success criteria, agreed now:** warns before at least [X]% of the breakdowns in the
  held-out period, with no more than [Y] false alarms per machine per month.
- **Timeline:** backtest results in 2 weeks, then [4–6] weeks of shadow mode if the
  backtest meets the criteria.
- **Data handling:** stays on your site or on hardware you approve, deleted at the end,
  covered by [your NDA / our agreement].

If this looks right, I'll send the data request and we can start.

[Your name]

---

## 4. If they ask "how accurate is it?"

> Honestly, I can't tell you for *your* machines until I test on your data. That's the
> point of the pilot. On public data from real equipment it ranges from strong (84% early
> warning on bearings, 20 of 20 turbine failures caught ahead of time) to still
> experimental (rooftop HVAC units). I'll show you the real numbers on your own history
> before you commit to anything.
